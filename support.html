Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a meal?

Open the app, tap the scan button, and take a photo of your meal (or choose one from your library). BrainFuel AI analyzes the photo and estimates how the meal will affect your focus, energy, and sleep.

Why did my scan fail or say the analyzer is busy?

This usually happens during periods of high demand on our AI analyzer. Wait a minute and try the scan again — it almost always succeeds on retry.

How does the free trial work?

New accounts get full Pro access — unlimited scans — free for the first 3 days, with no payment required. After that, the free plan includes 1 AI scan per day.

How do I subscribe, and what does Pro cost?

BrainFuel Pro is $9.99/month, purchased in-app through your Apple ID. Pro includes unlimited scans, metabolic mapping, and priority support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, select BrainFuel, and choose Cancel. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Is this medical advice?

No. BrainFuel's scores and suggestions are automated estimates generated from a photo — they are not medical, nutritional, or diagnostic advice. Always consult a qualified health professional before making decisions about your diet or health.
